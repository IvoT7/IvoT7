Work

KLIG business system

Invoicing, stock and point-of-sale software for KLIG, a company that sells car batteries to businesses and individuals. I built it from scratch, and it has been in daily use for over half a year.

  • Fiscal invoices for companies and individuals, with cancellations (storno) that keep a full audit trail
  • Point of sale for walk-in customers, with a daily turnover report in Excel for the accountant
  • Goods receipts and consignment stock placed with partner shops
  • A dashboard that brings invoices, shop sales and consignment cash into one revenue view
  • Automatic database backups
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • Node.js
  • Express
  • PostgreSQL
  • Electron

The code is private because the system runs a real company.

How stock works in KLIG

Nobody types the stock number in. Every delivery and sale is saved as a movement, and the quantity on hand is everything that came in minus everything that went out, so it always matches the history.
